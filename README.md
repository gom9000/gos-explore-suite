# Explore Suite
This suite is the most speculative corner of my workbench. It documents my passion for the sciences and my quest to observe and understand them through the tools I know.

>**[Mandelbrot Plots](https://github.com/gom9000/Mandelbrot_Plots)**<br/>
>**Type**: Fractal Geometry | **Status**: Completed
>
>An archive of high-resolution plots of the Mandelbrot and Julia sets generated with my custom software [Mandelbrot](https://github.com/gom9000/Mandelbrot). It documents the infinite detail of these mathematical structures through specific iteration and color mapping techniques.
